Fire Alarm and Detection Systems

Early warning that gives people time to get out safely, designed and installed for your building.

System Overview

A fire alarm and detection system spots smoke or heat at the earliest stage and alerts everyone in the building, giving people time to evacuate safely. ANTIFIRE designs, installs and maintains addressable and conventional systems for factories, hospitals, warehouses, offices and commercial buildings across Pakistan.

What’s Included

  • Site Risk Assessment
  • Custom System Design
  • Integration & Commissioning
  • Service & Support

Types of Systems

01Addressable

Each detector has its own address, so the panel shows the exact location of the alarm.

02Conventional

Detectors are wired in zones. A simple, cost-effective system for smaller buildings.

03Wireless

Radio-linked detectors for buildings where new wiring is difficult or not allowed.

04Aspirating Smoke Detection

Air-sampling detection that spots smoke extremely early, for server rooms and warehouses.

How It Works

Step 01Survey

We visit your site, assess the fire risks and map exactly where detectors, sounders and panels are needed.

Step 02Install

Our team installs the detectors, control panel, sounders and wiring to the agreed design.

Step 03Test

Every device is tested and the complete system is commissioned in front of your team.

Step 04Maintain

Scheduled servicing under a maintenance contract keeps the system tested, documented and ready.
