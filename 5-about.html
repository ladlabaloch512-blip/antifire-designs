Who We Are

Fire Safety Is All We Do

ANTIFIRE supplies fire protection and safety equipment to businesses, factories, warehouses, hospitals and commercial buildings across Pakistan, and stays responsible after the sale with installation, inspection, refilling and maintenance services.

Instead of dealing with separate suppliers, installers and service teams, you deal with one accountable team that knows your building and your systems.
